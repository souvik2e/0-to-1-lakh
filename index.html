The 3 mistakes that cost
us weeks of recovery

I made every mistake so you don't have to. These three are the most common among Bengali creators.

✗
Posting more than 1 reel per day

Instagram gives every account one daily distribution budget. Post 7 reels and that budget splits 7 ways. Our views collapsed overnight. Recovery took 14 days of strict discipline.

✗
Pitching big brands before having proof

Brand managers open your profile immediately after reading your email. If your recent reels show weak views, your email goes to trash — regardless of your all-time stats.

✗
Ignoring the 2-second hook

50% of viewers left in the first 2 seconds. We were opening with a static face and music — the most skipped opening on Instagram. High skip rate signals the algorithm to stop distribution.
