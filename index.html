The 3 mistakes that cost
us weeks of recovery

I made every mistake so you don't have to. These three are the most damaging — and the most common among Bengali creators.

✗
Posting more than 1 reel per day

Instagram gives every account one daily distribution budget. Post 7 reels and that budget splits 7 ways — each reel gets 1/7th the reach it deserved. Our views collapsed overnight. Recovery took 14 days of strict discipline. This single mistake is the most common growth killer among Bengali creators. Completely avoidable once you know about it.

✗
Pitching big brands before having proof

A brand manager receives 200+ pitches per week. They open your profile immediately after reading your email. If your recent reels show weak views, your email goes to trash — regardless of your all-time stats. The correct sequence: strong content first, then pitch. We wasted an entire month doing this backwards.

✗
Ignoring the 2-second hook

50% of viewers left in the first 2 seconds. We were opening with a static face and music — the most common and most skipped opening on Instagram. High skip rate signals the algorithm to stop distribution immediately. One hook change fixed this permanently. All 5 hook formulas in the guide pass this test.
